    How it began

    Born in a village. Called back to one.

    Mr. Potlapalli Balakrishna was born in Pullalacheruvu village in 1964 — the youngest of five brothers, raised in a family rooted in agriculture for generations. After a B.Sc. in Agriculture from Hindu College, Guntur, life took him to Hyderabad, where he built a career in teaching and real estate.

    But the soil never let go. In 2009, he made the call to go back. Not to retire — to build.

    "Work is worship. And there is no work more honest than raising food for the people you love."

    What started as a modest plot of land grew, slowly, into what it is today — a working farm with free-range goats, native chickens, dairy animals, and a small team that treats every animal like it matters. Because it does.

    His son, Venkateswara Rao, joined with the same conviction — and an unexpected rigour from his career in the pharmaceutical industry. Together, they run a farm that is equal parts traditional and modern.

    The Visionary · b. 1964

    Mr. Potlapalli Balakrishna

    Youngest of five brothers from Pullalacheruvu, Prakasam district. Educated in agriculture at Hindu College, Guntur, and trained further in History and Political Science at Osmania and Kakatiya Universities.

    After years of teaching and business in Hyderabad, he came back to do the one thing he had always known. In his own words, "work is worship" — and the farm is his cathedral.

    His vision is simple: a farm that keeps its word. Every price, every weight, every delivery — honest.

    The Founder · Hyderabad

    Mr. Potlapalli Venkateswara Rao

    A senior leader in the pharmaceutical industry, Venkateswara Rao brought something unusual to farming — scientific rigour. Hygiene, measurement, consistency. The same standards he applied to medicine, applied to food.

    He noticed something that bothered him: Hyderabad's meat supply was coming from Maharashtra, Karnataka, and beyond. Fresh didn't mean fresh. Local didn't mean local.

    So he set out to change that. Today, he oversees animal care, feed quality, and delivery logistics himself — building a farm that could serve the city honestly and at scale.
